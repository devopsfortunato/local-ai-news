n8n 3.0 pre-release: self-hosters should prep via Migration Report before the stable jump

Community benchmark: MTPLX beats mlx-serve and oMLX for Mac local inference, at a TTFT cost

A controlled r/LocalLLaMA comparison on an M5 Max (Inkling 2 Mini, same seeds/prompts) measured average decode of 85.0 / 73.8 / 57.6 tok/s for MTPLX / mlx-serve / oMLX, with vendor quant packs faster again. mlx-serve wins cold time-to-first-token (~1.5 s vs ~1.8 s), a practical tuning note for Mac homelab stacks.
