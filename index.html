Open-source UEFI tool lifts Nvidia's half-speed tensor cap on RTX 20/30/40 cards

The nv-uefi-unlocker project boots from a USB stick before the Nvidia driver loads and removes the FP16 tensor half-rate limiter on RTX 20, 30 and 40-series desktop and laptop GPUs, lifting matrix-math throughput by a reported 65-91% without VBIOS flashing or patched drivers. That is a measurable, free speedup for anyone running quantized LLMs on last-gen GeForce cards. Blackwell RTX 50-series cards remain unsupported, and the unlock must be reapplied after every cold boot.
