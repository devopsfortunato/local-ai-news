Meta opens the weights

Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an open-weight frontier multimodal MoE

Meta published the weights for Muse Spark Open, the open-weights edition of its August Muse Spark release: an 816B-parameter multimodal mixture-of-experts (42B active) that ingests text, images, audio and video. It ships with day-one support in Transformers, llama.cpp, vLLM, SGLang and MLX, plus long-context inference and fine-tuning via TRL. Meta added extra safety post-training so the model can run standalone without system guardrails, and pitches agentic capabilities targeting high-end desktop hardware.

NVIDIA readies the software stack as RTX Spark laptops ship

Ahead of the Oct 16 retail launch of RTX Spark laptops, NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B — a 74B MoE with 9B active parameters that fits in 24 GB via quantization-aware training, available through llama.cpp, vLLM, Ollama, Unsloth and LM Studio — and moved its PAIR distributed-inference router out of beta with llama.cpp support and RTX Spark integration.
